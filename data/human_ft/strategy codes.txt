    E.g. 3/5 + 2/5 = (3+2) / 5 = 5/5
    E.g. 3/5 * 2/5 = (3*2) / 5 = 6/5

2 Convert to Common Denominator, then Operate on Numerators, Keep Denominator. Follow the strategy described in 1 after attempting to convert the fractions to a common denominator.

    E.g. 3/5 + 1/2 = (3+1) / (5+2) = 4/7
    E.g. 3/5 * 1/2 = (3*1) / (5*2) = 3/10

6 Cross-Operate.
